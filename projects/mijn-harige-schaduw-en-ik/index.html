Mijn Harige Schaduw & Ik (My Hairy Shadow & I)

Directed by Andrei Mikes & Marc S. Nollkaemper | Mockumentary, Comedy, Fantasy | 9'50" | NL | 2024

Contribution: Editing, found footage design, storyboards, film poster

A film crew sets out to document Karel (27), who claims to be cursed by a hair fairy and is now being stalked by his own hairstyle.
